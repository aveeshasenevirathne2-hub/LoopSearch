How to Use LoopSearch

Instant Circle to Search workflow on Windows

1
Freeze Your Screen Anywhere Ctrl + Shift + Space

Press your global hotkey anytime or click the System Tray icon. Press Esc anytime to dismiss.

2
Switch Between 4 Precision Modes

Press keys 1–4 or click modes in the bottom glass capsule:

1 • Box Crop 2 • Freehand Loop 3 • Smart Tap 4 • Live OCR Text
3
Smart Action Chips & Offline Utilities

LoopSearch automatically detects URLs, QR codes, solves math expressions, picks dominant #HEX colors, runs offline Windows OCR, and answers with Gemini AI.

4
Pin Notes, Resize Sheet & Dark Web

Drag the Results header anywhere, resize edges, press P to pin a floating note above apps, or S to save PNG.
